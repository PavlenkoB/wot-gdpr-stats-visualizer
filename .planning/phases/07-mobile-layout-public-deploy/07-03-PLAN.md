<objective>
Give the shared chart tooltip a touch behaviour: tap opens and pins, another tap moves it, tap-again, tap-outside, Escape or scroll dismisses it, with the two fixed screen-reader announcements (PLATFORM-01; D-04, D-05, D-06). This plan builds the mechanism end to end on the hour grid and adds the touch Playwright project; plan 07-04 extends the same mechanism to the calendar, the bar charts and the map.

Purpose: the charts already work with a mouse and a keyboard, and a touch tap currently ends with no tooltip (the finger lifting fires pointerout, which closes what pointerover opened, and the cell's own focus event reopens it for a frame). The mechanism must not change either of those two paths.

Tracer: Task 1 is one real slice from the browser through the touch project to the hour grid's handlers and the shared tooltip, proven by one Playwright test. Task 2 adds the pin semantics, the announcements and the live region (pure logic first, with tests). Task 3 expands the hour-grid specs to every dismissal and the budget bookkeeping.
