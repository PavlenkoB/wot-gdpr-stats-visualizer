## Planner choices

- The per-section "scroll to each link moves the highlight to the matching link" wording in UI-SPEC's backstop is implemented as the mirror-the-rule assertion from RESEARCH Q5 finding 5 (the highlight is the section, or the last link when the page is at its bottom), because at Phase 3 content heights middle sections often cannot reach the observer band. Clicking User_Info may therefore highlight Not yet supported; that is expected, not a bug (RESEARCH Open Question 5).
- Reduced motion for the scroll tests: `test.use({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })` plus `behavior: 'instant'` scrolls, so smooth scrolling never makes them nondeterministic.
- The observer uses the UI-SPEC string exactly (`-72px 0px -60% 0px`, built from `--nav-offset`); computing the bottom margin in pixels and rebuilding on resize is deferred until Safari or Firefox testing exists (RESEARCH A1).
