## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Nav lookup.** The page has one nav element, so `scrollClear` finds it with a plain nav query and falls back to a zero bottom when absent. The nav's real bottom is measured, never assumed to be 56px (UI-SPEC checker flag).
- **Swatch size.** The UI-SPEC gives no swatch dimension; 12px squares are used (a multiple of 4 on the declared spacing scale) with the ramp variables as backgrounds.
- **Extra test ids.** `hour-scroller` and `playtime-charts` are additive to the UI-SPEC section 13 list and are used by the plan 04-11 specs.
- **Component verification.** Vitest has no DOM; the pure logic these components wire is unit tested in plans 04-04 and 04-05. Browser behaviour is checked by the Playwright specs in plan 04-11, which run in CI (Chromium cannot be downloaded in the cloud sandbox), and by the human checks below.
