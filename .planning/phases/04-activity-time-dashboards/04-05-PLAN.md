## Planner assumptions and flags

- **HOUR_GRID_BASIS (pending user decision).** The model takes the basis as an argument and defaults to the constant; both bases are tested.
- **Left and Right across a collapsed year.** The UI-SPEC says the move crosses into the adjacent year and stops at the first or last drawn day; it does not say what happens when the adjacent year is a collapsed empty run. This plan stops the move there (the target day must be drawn), and PageUp and PageDown are the keys that skip collapsed years, as the UI-SPEC's own PageUp row states.
- **Split table with a selection beyond the eighth value.** The table shows the first eight values; a selected value further down keeps the selector as its only indicator and no row carries the current-selection marker. The live-region announcement names it.
- **Tooltip flip rule.** "Not enough room above" is implemented as: the tooltip's top edge when placed above would be less than navOffset plus 8px from the viewport top.
