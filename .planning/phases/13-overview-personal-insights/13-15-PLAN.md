<objective>
Pass the Terms-of-Use gate, then generate and commit the real tank table. This is the phase's one-way step (D-07): once vehicle data is committed to the public repository it cannot be withdrawn without a history rewrite and a fresh repository. A person reads the licence, decides the attribution and approves; the maintainer runs the generator with their own key; only then does the executor verify the output, set the approved attribution and commit.

Purpose: INSIGHT-06 and D-07, D-08, D-09, D-23. The generator, the empty module, the lookup, the worker-only rule and the footer already exist (plans 13-02 and 13-12); this plan supplies the legal approval, the data and the proof that it works.

Output: the approved attribution constants, the generated table, committed-table tests and one browser test showing real classes resolve.

This plan's first task is the blocking Terms gate that CONTEXT D-23 calls a human-verify task and the planning request asks for. It is typed `checkpoint:decision` with `gate="blocking-human"` for two reasons: the project's `workflow.human_verify_mode` is `end-of-phase`, which suppresses planner-emitted human-verify tasks (decision and human-action checkpoints are unaffected), and a legal reading that precedes a one-way commit gates the work itself rather than verifying finished work. Its behaviour is the one D-23 asks for: a person reads the licence, the answer carries the attribution, and a blocking-human gate is never auto-approved. The one-way choice (D-07) was settled by the human in CONTEXT; this checkpoint is the Terms gate that choice requires, so it doubles as the confirmation before the one-way commit and no second decision checkpoint is added.
