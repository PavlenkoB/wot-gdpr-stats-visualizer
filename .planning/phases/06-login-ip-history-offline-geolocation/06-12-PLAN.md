<objective>
Render the country map: a hand-rolled SVG shaded by login count on a logarithmic scale, with a tooltip, a one-stop keyboard model, a ranked country list that carries every number as text, and the DB-IP attribution.

Purpose: ACTIVITY-06 asks for country-level geolocation on a bundled world-map SVG with an attribution line, and success criterion 4 asks that the map stay responsive by aggregating per country. The map never receives an address, so nothing here can leak one, and its node count follows the country count, never the login count.

Tracer note: Task 1 is the slice: the real view shades one real country path in the browser through the pure level function. Interaction, legend, attribution, list and states follow in Task 2.

Output: `map-model.ts`, `WorldMap.svelte`, `CountryList.svelte`, `LoginMapSection.svelte`, the block wiring and the tests.
</objective>
