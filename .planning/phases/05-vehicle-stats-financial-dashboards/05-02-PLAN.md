## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Only some of the 47 WoT columns are reproduced.** The research recorded the names of the columns Phase 5 reads, not all 47. The fixture header carries those plus ACHIEVEMENTS and FRAGS (the two columns with blank cells in the real sample). The builder must tolerate absent columns anyway (CONTEXT, Defensive parsing), and the fixture is not a byte copy of any real file.
- **Vehicle ids outside WoT.** Only WoT ids were verified to carry a `nation:` prefix (RESEARCH A9). The Blitz and Warships fixtures deliberately mix prefixed and unprefixed ids so the generic split-on-first-colon rule is exercised both ways.
- **NAME canary.** The account nickname column is named NAME in every account file. The canary value is one distinctive string so a leak is greppable in DOM text, attributes and every worker message.
- **Independent of plan 05-01.** `renderStatsCsv` is written here rather than imported from the finance fixtures, because both plans run in wave 1 and neither may depend on the other.
