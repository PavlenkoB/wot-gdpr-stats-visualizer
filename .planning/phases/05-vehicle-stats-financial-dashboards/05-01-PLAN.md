## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Where the rows are written out.** The premium and orders rows are listed below as prose because their totals are derived from them; the currency set is formulaic (a closed-form loop) because the 50-operation chart threshold needs at least 50 rows per charted code.
- **Plausible-date window (planner decision, flagged).** A CREATED or ACTION_DT value is "readable" only when it parses (ISO or dotted day-first) AND its year is between 1970 and 2100 inclusive. The UI-SPEC says a stray year lengthens the axis honestly; the window bounds that at 1,572 months so one crafted cell cannot become a hundred-thousand-node chart (T-05-08, owned by plan 05-04). A row outside the window is an excluded row for the "date cannot be read" clause that plan 05-08 adds to the approved pill copy. The fixtures below include the year 9999 and the year 1969 cases that exercise it.
- **Blank PREMIUM.** A blank PREMIUM cell is an excluded row that is counted but has no string to list.
- **Row order within the tooltip's grant sizes.** Most frequent first; ties by size ascending. The hand fixture records this in `PREMIUM_HAND_EXPECTED`.
- **Mulberry32.** The seeded PRNG is imported from `tests/fixtures/sessions-fixtures.ts` (it is exported there); do not copy it.
