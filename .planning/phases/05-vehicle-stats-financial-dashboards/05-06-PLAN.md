## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Quote-all, bare-CR sample files.** The new Player_statistics, Financial_data and Orders files are written exactly as the fixture modules render them (header unquoted, quote-all data, bare CR), unlike the other sample files, which keep their CRLF style. That mix is deliberate: the real export mixes line endings too (PROJECT.md), and the Phase 2 parser must keep handling both.
- **Template rows for the scale export.** The scale templates take their header and first data row from the first two bare-CR lines of the matching fixture text, so no new export from the fixture modules is needed.
- **Why e2e specs change little.** Plan 05-03 already made them derive supported ids and offsets from constants; this plan only has to confirm they still pass and fix any literal a run exposes.
- **Row count arithmetic.** Expected values: Player_statistics 19 files and 47 rows; Financial_data 2 files and 143 rows (18 premium rows and 125 currency rows); Payment_history 1 file and 16 rows. They come from the fixture modules; the shape test re-derives them by parsing.
