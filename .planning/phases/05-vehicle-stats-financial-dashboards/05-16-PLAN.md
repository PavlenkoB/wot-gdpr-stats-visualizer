## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Budget rule (deterministic).** With L the number printed by `playwright test --list` and E the projection from the updated constants: globalTimeout is the whole number of minutes `ceil(1.3 * E / 60) + 3` (the three minutes of headroom Phase 4 used), never below the current 15; the shell wrapper is globalTimeout plus 2 minutes; the job timeout is the wrapper plus 8 minutes. At the 109 tests Phase 4 measured (E = 540 seconds) the rule gives 15, 17 and 25 minutes, the figures Phase 4 set, and it keeps the three orderings the unit test enforces.
- **Scale tests share one import.** One serial describe imports the 50,000-row export once; the 10,000-row comparison import is a second small import in the same describe.
- **Vehicle scale is already covered.** The 5,000-vehicle windowing proof ships with plan 05-12; this plan adds only the finance scale proof and the zero-network and budget work.
- **Premium rows scale identically.** The premium builder buckets by month and the month span is bounded by the date window, so its cost is covered by the currency run; a separate premium generator is not needed.
- **Real-browser timing is evidence, not a gate.** The measured import-to-ready seconds are recorded in the SUMMARY; the spec asserts a generous ceiling so a slow runner does not fail it, in the same way Phase 4's scale spec did.
