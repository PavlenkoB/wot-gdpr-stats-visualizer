<objective>
Rename the naming trap: the shared muted 14px note (`.caption`) and the 12px type role (`.t-caption`) have near-identical names and different meanings. The 12px role keeps its name because roles follow their token family (`--fs-caption`) and the role is used by the frozen Overview; the utility is the odd one out and is the one renamed. Decision: `.muted-note`. It is not `.note` (14 local hook classes already use that name next to the utility) and not `.hint` (9 do), and it does not start with `t-`, which the shared-name guard reserves for roles.

Purpose: stop two classes of different size sharing a word, in a way a scanner can prove and a build cannot get wrong.
Output: renamed utility, 22 components, 3 unit tests, 2 e2e specs, guard lists and a scanner, with the old name gone from `src/` and `tests/`.
</objective>
