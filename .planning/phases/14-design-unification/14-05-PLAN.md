<objective>
Migrate the shell's titles: the pane title becomes one role (D-12), the Overview takes that role without moving anything below it (D-16), and the identity header joins the token layer.

This is the first plan with intended pixel changes, so it owns the closed Allowed Visible Changes for titles: 26px to 32px at desktop widths and 18px to 26px on a phone (OI-1, OI-2, OI-3). Research found the trap in this change: the phone block in `app.css` sets titles to 18px with selectors of specificity (0,3,0), which would beat `.t-title` (0,1,0); those declarations are deleted, not overridden. The second trap is the Overview (D-15 against D-12): its title gets the same 32px, whose 1.3 line box is 7.8px taller than today's, which would push every tile down and change the size of the whole page. The owner decided (D-16, 2026-10-25) that the Overview compensates: a negative bottom margin of exactly that difference keeps the title block's row height, so content below the title does not move and every Overview element keeps its size. The other panes' title rows grow by about 8px, which is allowed.

Purpose: D-12 is the only decision here that changes what a reader sees on every pane; doing it on the shell first lets every later pane plan assume titles are done, and D-16 keeps the Overview freeze (D-15) honest from the first plan that touches it.
Output: `.t-title` on the pane, identity and phone-bar titles, the Overview compensation rule with its unit test, and migrated `DashboardSection`, `Overview`, `DetailPane` and `IdentityHeader`.
</objective>
