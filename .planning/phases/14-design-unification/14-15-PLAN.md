<objective>
Migrate the chart pieces that the finance pane shares with the rest of the app: the flow legend, the tooltip, the bar chart, the monthly table and the About disclosure.

Two traps are specific to this group. The bar chart's `.scroller` is a chart scroller (not a focusable region), so it is renamed to the local `chart-scroll` hook and must not gain the shared region's isolation; and `about-disclosure.test.ts` loops over `font-size: Npx` matches, which would find nothing after tokenising and pass vacuously unless its assertion reads resolved token values and requires a non-empty match.

Purpose: `ChartTooltip` is also used by the playtime charts, so these pieces land before the finance blocks (plan 14-16) and the Overview.
Output: five migrated files, one reader hardened against a vacuous pass, and the `#ffffff` allow-list entry gone.
</objective>
