<objective>
Migrate the three optional game-data panes (HEAT, Perks, Warships crews and storage) and their two shared pieces, the card and the table.

This group carries the most literal-CSS assertions of the phase (`new-data-blocks.test.ts` asserts the card's border, radius, padding and gap, the 40px rows, the scroller ring, the `dl` grid and the caption colour as source text), so the plan's real work is keeping each assertion's meaning while the source changes form. One of them, the font-size sweep, has no length check today and would pass with an empty match once the sizes are tokens; it gets one.

Purpose: all three panes are the "new data" of milestone v1.1 and share a card; migrating them together keeps the card's two consumers consistent.
Output: five migrated components and three test files moved onto the token-aware helpers.
</objective>
