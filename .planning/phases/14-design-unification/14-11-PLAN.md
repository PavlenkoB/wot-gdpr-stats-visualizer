Purpose: after this plan no 30px size exists, so `SCALE_PX` drops its temporary member and the type scale is exactly the six sizes of D-06. Plan 14-12 finishes the pane (the city opt-in and the history block).
Output: five migrated components, `SCALE_PX` at its final value, the allowlists shortened.
