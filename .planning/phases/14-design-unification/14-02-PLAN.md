<objective>
Give every token one home, and pin the new heat contract before any pane depends on it.

Plan 14-01 created `src/styles/tokens.css` with the new families only; the legacy palette, the flow and Overview colours, the heat stops and the Activity ramp still sit in `src/app.css`. This plan moves them verbatim (D-02: tokens in `src/styles/`, `app.css` keeps element resets and layout), after first making the six tests that read token values location-agnostic so the move is a pure relocation with the suite green on both sides of it. It then adds the heat contract (D-11 as amended: five data levels `--heat-1` to `--heat-5` plus a neutral `--heat-empty`, 3:1 on the five data steps, strictly monotonic lightness across all six swatches, adjacent-step floor 1.1:1) to `contrast.test.ts`: the derived steps are written as `color-mix(in srgb, ...)`, so the test mirrors the arithmetic in the same colour space, asserts the declared strings so a silent switch to another space fails, and asserts the contrast and monotonic-lightness rules of the approved UI-SPEC.

Purpose: plans 14-09 (heat consumers) and 14-19 to 14-20 (Overview) assert against a contract that already exists and already fails when it is violated.
Output: `src/styles/tokens.css` holding every token, a slimmer `src/app.css`, five repointed readers, a narrowed `readTokenSource`, and the heat describe block.
</objective>
