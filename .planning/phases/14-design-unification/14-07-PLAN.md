<objective>
Migrate the first half of the shell chrome: the app root and import bar that draw the spinner, and the four status surfaces (failure banner, exclusion notice, import summary, confirm panel). This is the plan that retires the status-colour hex literals from the shell and the last two `rgba()` spinner copies (the Overview's three shadows follow in plan 14-19).

Nothing here is meant to change a pixel: the status tokens carry the exact values of the literals they replace, `color-mix(in srgb, var(--focus) 25%, transparent)` computes to the same channels and alpha as `rgba(26, 115, 232, 0.25)` (probe-verified in 14-RESEARCH Pattern 3), and the shared `.caption` equals the local copy it replaces.

Purpose: D-01 and D-03 for the shell chrome. Plan 14-08 finishes the remaining shell pieces.
Output: 6 migrated files, two new tokens, two phase-8 guard edits that keep the old rules in step.
</objective>
