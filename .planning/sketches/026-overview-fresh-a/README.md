# Sketch 026: Fresh alternatives A

## How to View
`node .planning/sketches/_shared/gen-data.mjs` once, then open `.planning/sketches/026-overview-fresh-a/index.html`.

## Variants
- **H: Small multiples (years)** - six rows (logged hours, sessions, typical session, days played, evening share, weekend share), each a 16-bar year series with its own peak, the current year striped as partial; hover or a year slider (keyboard/phone) reads one year across all rows. Sources: small-multiples/sparkline practice (Tufte).
- **K: A typical week** - 7x24 weekday-by-hour heatmap with the peak cell outlined (Friday 17:00), weekday bars, weekend share and small-hours share. Reuses the Playtime hour-grid idea.
- **M: Your top 5s** - six ranked lists (tanks, years, days, nations, hours of day, tiers) with bars, rank numbers and a rule line each.

## What to Look For
- H: does seeing 14 years in six rows reveal change better than any single number? Is the slider/hover readout enough, or too analytic?
- K: is the weekly rhythm a good first thing to see, or better as a chapter?
- M: familiar "Top 5" format, easy to scan; does it feel personal or generic?
