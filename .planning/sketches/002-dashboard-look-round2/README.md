# Sketch 002: Dashboard Look, Round 2

## Research basis
- Bento grids: varied tile size = importance (Linear, Datadog, Apple) - saasframe.io/patterns/bento-grid
- Wrapped / Year-in-Review: big-number story panels, themed to player (Xbox Year in Review, Spotify Wrapped)
- 2026 dashboard guidance: lower cognitive load, remove anything not supporting the decision (adminlte.io 2026 dashboard principles)

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-dashboard-look-round2/index.html

## Variants
- **D: Bento** - dark violet, mixed-size tiles, hero tile for playtime, KPI row
- **E: Wrapped Story** - full-screen scroll-snap panels, one stat per screen, vivid gradients, shareable feel
- **F: Tactical Briefing** - olive/amber WoT-flavored, one section per tab (no long scroll), stencil headings

## What to Look For
D: density vs round 1. E: delight vs usefulness (tables/filters live where?). F: does tabbing fix the long page?
