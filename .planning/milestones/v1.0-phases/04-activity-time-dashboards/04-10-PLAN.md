## Planner assumptions and flags

- **Year head wrapping.** Rather than a breakpoint, the head is a wrapping flex row with a 32px minimum height: on wide screens the summary sits on the same line as the year, on narrow ones it wraps below. Its real height is measured at focus time, so both cases clear correctly (resolves the UI-SPEC checker's 32px-versus-48px flag without a hard-coded number).
- **Ring room.** The 10px cells leave no room inside for a 4px two-tone ring, so the scroller has 4px vertical padding and the svg allows overflow; the calendar keeps the specified 626 by 97 svg size.
- **Additive attributes.** `data-day` (day index) and `data-seconds` are additive to UI-SPEC section 13 and are used by the keyboard handler and the plan 04-11 and 04-12 specs. Neither carries anything but a date and a duration.
- **Component verification.** Vitest has no DOM; the logic this plan wires (geometry, levels, navigation) is unit tested in plan 04-05, and browser behaviour is asserted by plan 04-11 in CI (Chromium cannot be downloaded in the cloud sandbox) and by the human checks below.
