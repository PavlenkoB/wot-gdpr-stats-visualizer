<objective>
Apply the UI-SPEC's "About this chart" disclosure pattern to the chart blocks without hiding any copy an earlier phase requires to be visible, and bring the stat tiles to the Day 10 numeral and chrome tokens.

Purpose: the approved UI-SPEC copy row ("wraps existing explanatory copy from each block") and typography table, reconciled with Phase 4 D-01/D-05 and the map's licence attribution.
Output: `AboutDisclosure.svelte`, its copy and guard tests, five chart components updated, two tile stylesheets updated.

Decision to flag in the summary: the UI-SPEC says captions move into the details, but D-01 (Phase 4) says the basis statement must be visible text under the chart, and the map attribution is a licence obligation. So the disclosure wraps only the supplementary copy: the keyboard-use hints (they are `aria-describedby` targets and stay in the DOM) and the conditional hour-grid start-basis note. This is the narrowest reading that satisfies both documents.
</objective>
