<objective>
Extend the touch mechanism built in plan 07-03 to the remaining three chart surfaces: the monthly bar charts, the year calendar and the world map (PLATFORM-01; D-05; D-02 for the map).

Purpose: tapping any chart readout works the same way, and the two pieces that differ are handled deliberately: the bar charts share one tooltip between two charts (a tap must be able to move across them), and the map anchors its tooltip at a pointer position rather than on a cell (so the touch path must give the shared tooltip a real element to compare against).

Tracer: Task 1 is the bar charts, chosen first because they are the case most unlike the hour grid: a different tooltip owner (the Financial block), two charts on one tooltip, and the announcement reaching the page-level region without any new prop. If the plan 07-03 mechanism holds there, the calendar and the map are the same pattern.

Execution constraints (decisions already made, none reopened):
- D-05: touch only; no timers; mouse and keyboard unchanged. D-02: the map only scales; no pinch-zoom, pan or min-width. D-06: no new component or dependency.
- The pure decision module, `ChartTooltip.svelte` and `Dashboard.svelte` are plan 07-03's and are NOT edited here. Each chart only gains: touch early returns in its over and out handlers, a touch branch in its pointer-down handler, and the `data-tooltip-surface` attribute on its root.
- Existing source-contract tests read these files and constrain the edits (read them before editing): `tests/unit/playtime/playtime-charts.test.ts` requires the calendar's `onPointerOver` to assign `hoverDay` from the found day and NOT mention `keyboardFocus`, `onPointerOut` to clear `hoverDay`, and `onPointerDown` to never mention `hoverDay`; `tests/unit/charts/bar-chart-components.test.ts` requires `onpointerover=`, `role="grid"` and `role="gridcell"` and forbids `viewBox`, `{@html`, a `style=` attribute and a `style:` directive in the comment-stripped bar chart; `tests/unit/logins/map-model.test.ts` forbids, in the comment-stripped map component, any `style` attribute or directive, the word `transition` (case-insensitive), animation declarations and `{@html`, and requires the `var(--ramp-N)` tokens and the static map import; `tests/unit/guards/phase6-guards.test.ts` applies a no-motion and no-inline-style scan to every logins component.
- The three tooltip test ids are `chart-tooltip` (Playtime charts container), `finance-tooltip` (Financial block, shared by both bar charts) and `map-tooltip` (the map). Specs scope to the right one.
- CI e2e budget: the formula is in plan 07-03 Task 3 and the guard enforces it; this plan adds three tests and the total will pass 239, so Task 3 raises the three limits together.
- `touch-tooltips.spec.ts` must import `../_support/pin-locale` before anything else once it imports a formatter such as the calendar date formatter from `ui-copy` (a unit guard in `tests/unit/shell/ui-copy.test.ts` fails any e2e spec that imports the copy module unpinned).
