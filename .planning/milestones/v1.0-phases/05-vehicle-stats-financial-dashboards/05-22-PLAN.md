<objective>
Close the width clause of UAT gap G-05-9, the last open part of it, by the user's decision (option B of the options put to them after plan 05-21 was written): a finer integer pitch. Together with plan 05-21 (colours and the empty-axis split) this closes G-05-9 in full.

Why only a finer pitch. Every chart spans its own months (D-17, locked: "from the first operation"), so equal widths would need a shared month axis (not chosen, it would amend D-17) or a stretched frame (rejected: a flat zero line across months that are not in the data reads as "nothing happened"). The old pitch ladder of 16, 12, 8 and 4px wastes plot width in steps: measured on a real export, a 56-month chart used 448 of 576px, and a 73-month chart would use 292. A whole-pixel pitch fixes most of that without changing what the axis means.

The rule: pitch is the largest whole number from 4 to 16 for which months x pitch is at most 576, and bar width stays pitch - 1. Hand-derived: 37 to 38 months give 15, 39 to 41 give 14, 42 to 44 give 13, 45 to 48 give 12, 49 to 52 give 11, 53 to 57 give 10, 58 to 64 give 9, 65 to 72 give 8, 73 to 82 give 7, 83 to 96 give 6, 97 to 115 give 5, 116 to 144 give 4. The plot is then 80.6% to 100% full for 37 to 144 months (the user quoted about 95%, which is the typical figure, not the minimum; the worst case is 116 months at 464px, because pitch 4 is the floor). Months up to 36 keep pitch 16 (a bar is a mark, not a panel, so a 5-month chart stays narrow) and months over 144 keep pitch 4 and scroll.

The label stride has to follow, because the old rule only knew pitch 4: with pitches 5 to 7, a label every 12 months would be 60 to 84px apart, under the UI-SPEC's 96px. The stride is 12 when 12 x pitch is at least 96 (pitch 8 or more) and 24 otherwise; 24 x 4 is exactly 96, so the floor holds at every pitch.

Output: the model change with hand-derived tests that fail on the old ladder, one browser proof, and a dated UI-SPEC amendment appendix. The component, the currency plot split, the colours and the premium plot height are untouched (plan 05-21 owns them).

Deliberately NOT done here: a shared month axis, frame stretching, raising the 16px cap for short charts, and any change to D-15 or D-17.
