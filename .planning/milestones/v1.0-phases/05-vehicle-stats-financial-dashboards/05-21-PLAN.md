Measured live against a synthetic export before planning: the chart zero line computes to rgb(218, 220, 224) (1.37:1) and the hairlines to the off-palette rgb(232, 234, 237); the contract says zero line #5f6368 (6.05:1) and hairlines #dadce0. The zero line is the non-colour cue the contract leans on to let the two flow hues sit 1.36:1 apart, so this is a real contract miss, not decoration. No test checked either colour.

Empty-axis waste, the decision taken here and why. The diverging chart gives deposits and withdrawals 80px each way on ONE shared scale, so a currency that is almost all deposits wastes its lower half and its large negative label floats far from any bar. Three ways to fix that were weighed:
- Give each side its own scale: rejected, because a 10-unit withdrawal would then be drawn as tall as a 1,000,000 deposit, and the chart's one purpose (deposit and withdrawal bars in the same chart are directly comparable) would be gone.
- Shrink the plot to what the smaller side needs: rejected, because chart height would then differ chart to chart and every height assertion would move.
- CHOSEN: keep the plot at 160px (the SVG stays 192px), keep ONE scale (the same pixels per unit above and below zero), and divide the 160px between the sides in proportion to each side's extent. A side's extent is its own largest month, but never less than a quarter of the other side's extent; that floor is 32px, so the smaller side stays readable and the 14px labels cannot collide. The chart still has its own scale (D-15; the block caption saying bar heights cannot be compared BETWEEN charts stays true). This rule supersedes the approved UI-SPEC's "symmetric" wording, so Task 3 adds a dated amendment appendix; the original text is left in place as history.

Not in this plan: G-05-9's third clause (charts in one card share a width and fill it). Each chart's month axis is its own span (D-17, locked: "from the first operation"), so equal widths cannot be had by padding or stretching the frame (that draws a flat zero line across months that are not in the data, which reads as "nothing happened"). The user chose a finer integer pitch (largest whole pixel up to 16 with months x pitch no more than 576) over a shared month axis and over leaving it; that is planned as 05-22 (wave 2, after this plan, because both edit bar-chart-model.ts, its test, finance.spec.ts, contrast.test.ts and the UI-SPEC).
