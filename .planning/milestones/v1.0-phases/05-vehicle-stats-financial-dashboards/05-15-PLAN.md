<objective>
Make the Payment_history card live with the real-money spend summary: one card per currency with the paid total as the headline, the separate failed, new and gift figures, the collapsed per-year and per-game breakdowns and the excluded-order pill, and nothing anywhere that blends currencies.

Purpose: FINANCE-03's roadmap criterion 4 is a negative one (no blended or converted grand total anywhere), so it is proved on a multi-currency fixture by structure, not by the absence of a word. The exact arithmetic, the grouping and the ordering are plan 05-04's; this plan renders them faithfully and proves the roadmap criterion in a browser.

Output: `SpendSummary.svelte`, the status flip with migrated Phase 3 assertions, a source-contract test and the spend browser specs.

Decisions implemented here: D-21 (per-currency PAID total and count, FAILED and NEW separate, no blended figure), D-22 (per-year and per-game breakdowns, expandable), D-23 (gifts included and shown separately), D-24 (code as written, two decimals, unknown currency, non-numeric excluded and counted).
</objective>
