<objective>
Build the opt-in city dataset end to end on the data side: the Node script that generates it, and the worker code that, given the bytes, answers a city view for the current dataset. End with the record of the city-fetch decision (RESEARCH Decision 3) that gates plan 06-11.

Purpose: ACTIVITY-07 needs a separate, much larger offline city database that is never bundled or downloaded by default (D-13). The measured compact form is about 11.85 MB gzipped, not 60 to 70 MB. The worker side is independent of where the bytes are fetched: whichever thread fetches, the worker receives a buffer.

Tracer note: Task 1 is the slice: a fixture CSV through the real build script into a gzipped container, posted to the real worker, answered as a city view for fixture addresses.

Output: `build-city-dataset.ts`, the columnar encoder, `city-table.ts`, `city-shared.ts`, the worker messages and handler, the fixture, and tests. The city fetch, CSP and lint exemption are plan 06-11 and depend on the checkpoint at the end of this plan.
