The sketch's behaviour (.claude/skills/sketch-findings-wot-gdpr-stats-visualizer/sources/overview-final/030-overview-final/index.html, the script from the `vertical` function through the keydown handler; read it before porting): `vertical(cur, dir)` iterates every visible toggle button; for tile rect r (current) and q (candidate) it computes dy = dir > 0 ? q.top - r.bottom : r.top - q.bottom and skips dy below -1; skips overlap = min(r.right, q.right) - max(r.left, q.left) at or below 1; keeps the candidate with the smaller dy (by more than 1), or equal dy (within 1) and a larger overlap (strictly), so the earlier tile in DOM order wins an exact tie. `anchored(el, mutate)` records the tile's top before, runs mutate (inside document.startViewTransition(run) when the API exists and reduced motion is off), restores focus if the active element was dropped, then scrolls by (after - before) when it differs by more than 1; `setTimeout(run, 600)` is the safety net and `run` is guarded by a `done` flag; the promises ready, finished and updateCallbackDone are all caught. `setOpen(tile, open)` records the wanted state first (data-want), then applies; a click toggles from the recorded want, not from the DOM. Each tile is open or closed independently (several tiles may be open). Escape closes the focused tile only when its wanted state is open, so Escape on a closed tile falls through to the pane.
