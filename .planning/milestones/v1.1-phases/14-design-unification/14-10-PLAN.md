<objective>
Finish the playtime pane: the summary tiles (D-08, the first of the two 30px numerals), the pane's own block and the charts container.

The numeral is the only intended pixel change here. D-08 snaps 30px to the Display role (26px), so `PlaytimeSummary` takes `.t-display` and its `dd` rule stops declaring size, weight and line height; the 375px size stays 18px because the phone block's `.shell .playtime-summary dd` rule (specificity 0,2,1) outranks the role. The Playtime block also owns three of the shared names (`.caption` with a 1.5 line height, `.body`, `.error`) and the cache-cleared notice, which is a warning box in status hex today.

Purpose: playtime is the busiest pane of the app; once it is on tokens, only the logins numeral (14-11) remains of the 30px sites.
Output: three migrated components, an amended e2e numeral assertion, and the playtime reader tests moved onto the token-aware helpers.
</objective>
