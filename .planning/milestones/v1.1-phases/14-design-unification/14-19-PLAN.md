<objective>
Bring the Overview tile onto the shared tokens without changing a pixel of it (D-09, D-15): tokens, the exact-match roles and the three elevation tokens.

The Overview is the design system the rest of the app is joining, so this plan inverts the usual risk: the only way to fail is to change what it already looks like. Its sizes (12, 14, 32, 56) are already the new scale and its spacing is already on the 4/8/12/16/24 scale apart from one 32px, so the migration is expressed in tokens and exact-match roles; the three `rgba()` shadows become `color-mix(in srgb, var(--ink) N%, transparent)` tokens (probe-verified to compute to the same channels and alpha). The arbiter is the scratch byte-for-byte compare of the Overview root against the pre-phase build, with the title box masked and the sizes equal (D-16): where adopting a role at a site changes a pixel, that site keeps its original px through token declarations.

Purpose: D-15, the requirement that makes the rest of the phase a convergence onto the Overview rather than a redesign. Plan 14-20 migrates the other four Overview components and records the result.
Output: a migrated `OverviewTile`, three elevation tokens, and a contract test that reads tokens and roles.
</objective>
