<objective>
Make every reader of stored rows account-aware, and prove the one-dataset lifecycle (reload, duplicate, replace, clear, abort) with one, two and three accounts.

Purpose: ACCOUNT-03 says one cached dataset holds all accounts with replace-on-new-upload and hash-dedupe behaving as before; ACCOUNT-08 says Clear and Start Over wipe all accounts and the fixtures cover one, two and three roots. The import path stores accounts since plan 09-02; this plan makes the lazy rebuilds equal the import (so a deleted aggregate comes back identical), keeps the login pane from presenting one account's record as everyone's, and pins the lifecycle on fake IndexedDB so the browser plan needs only a few specs.

This plan also finishes the privacy half of roadmap SC4 that plan 09-02 starts: D-18 stops new imports from storing unknown and known-deferred rows, and D-21 removes the rows a returning v1.0 cache already holds (for example the Game_Forums EMAIL column), on load, in the worker. It is placed here and not in plan 09-02 because 09-02 is at 90k of the 100k budget and this plan already owns `db.ts` and the cache-lifecycle tests.

Output: account-level grouping and the one-account pool rule in `db.ts`, the load-time purge `purgeUnsupportedCategoryRows` called from the check-cache handler, and the lifecycle test matrix. No UI change.

Decisions implemented here: D-07, D-08, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-21.

Project rules honoured: no dependency, no network call, no Dexie version change, no Co-Authored-By trailer (AGENTS.md).
